ABOUT US

Welcome to Nila Hotel, where comfort, elegance, and warm hospitality come together to create an unforgettable stay. Established with a vision of providing exceptional accommodation and personalized service, our hotel has grown into a welcoming destination for families, couples, business travelers, and holiday guests. From beautifully designed rooms and delicious dining experiences to modern amenities and relaxing spaces, every detail is carefully planned to make your stay comfortable and memorable. Our convenient location also allows guests to easily explore the beauty, culture, and attractions surrounding the hotel. Whether you are visiting for a relaxing vacation, a business trip, or a special celebration, we are committed to making every moment of your stay special. With comfortable rooms, complimentary Wi-Fi, a restaurant, fitness facilities, spa services, room service, and dedicated staff, Serene Grand Hotel offers everything you need for a truly enjoyable experience.

Our services

Relax and refresh your body and mind with our professional spa and wellness services. Enjoy a peaceful atmosphere, relaxing treatments, and personalized care designed to help you unwind after a busy day.

Stay active during your stay at our modern fitness center. Equipped with essential workout equipment, our gym provides a convenient space for guests to maintain their fitness routine while traveling.

Enjoy delicious meals prepared by our experienced chefs using fresh and quality ingredients. Our restaurant offers a variety of local and international dishes, while room service allows you to enjoy your favorite meals from the comfort of your room.

Stay connected throughout your visit with complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi available across the hotel. Whether you need to attend an online meeting, stay connected with family, or simply browse the internet, reliable connectivity is available whenever you need it.

Our room service is available around the clock to provide you with food, beverages, and other essential services directly to your room. Our team is always ready to assist with your requests and make your stay more convenient.

Guests can enjoy convenient parking facilities during their stay. We can also assist with transportation arrangements, taxi services, and local travel requirements to help you explore nearby attractions comfortably.

Our housekeeping team maintains high standards of cleanliness and hygiene throughout the hotel. Daily room cleaning, fresh linens, towels, and other essential services ensure that your room remains clean and comfortable

For business travelers, we provide useful facilities such as workspaces, internet access, printing assistance, and meeting areas. Our services are designed to help you stay productive and connected while away from the office.

Make your special occasions memorable with our event and celebration facilities. From weddings and birthday parties to corporate meetings and private gatherings, our team can help organize and manage your event according to your requirements.

We provide comfortable facilities and services for families traveling with children. Spacious rooms, family-friendly dining, and attentive assistance help ensure that guests of all ages enjoy a pleasant and stress-free stay.
